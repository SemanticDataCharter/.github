# Contributor License Agreements

Axius SDC, Inc. asks every contributor to agree to a Contributor License Agreement before their
first contribution is merged into a repository in the SemanticDataCharter organization.

- **[Individual CLA](ICLA.md)** for a person contributing on their own behalf.
- **[Corporate CLA](CCLA.md)** for a company or other legal entity whose employees or agents
  contribute in the course of their work.

## Why

**You keep ownership of your work.** A CLA is not an assignment. Nothing in it limits what you may
do with your own contribution anywhere else.

**What it grants us is the right to sublicense.** Axius SDC maintains open source projects under
Apache License 2.0 and also distributes commercial and sovereign-deployment products built on the
same codebase. Without an explicit sublicense right, a contribution accepted into an open repository
could not lawfully be carried into those products. Stating that up front is more honest than
discovering it later.

**It protects you too.** The agreement records that you had the right to contribute what you
contributed, which is the question that actually gets asked years later when nobody remembers.

## How

**Individuals**: open a pull request as normal. On your first one, post this comment:

> I have read the Axius SDC Individual Contributor License Agreement and I hereby accept its terms.

Include your full legal name and the GitHub username you contribute under. That covers all your
future contributions.

**Organizations**: email a completed [Corporate CLA](CCLA.md), including Schedule A, to
legal@axius-sdc.com from an address at your domain.
